About Me

The person behind the code

I'm Ankit Gupta, a Design Verification Engineer with 4+ years of experience closing verification for complex ASIC products at Chelsio Communications, Bangalore. My days are spent with SystemVerilog, UVM, and chasing down elusive PCIe bugs at Gen5 speeds.

But I don't stop there. By night I'm an indie builder — I created DV Architect, a visual UVM IDE that makes testbench design drag-and-drop. I also build web apps: a Shopify analytics tool, an anonymous chat app, and more.

I studied Electronics & Telecommunication at VIIT, Pune, where I was Technical Head of the IoT Forum, CMA Head of Lighthouse Magazine, and won national-level competitions. I hold 2 granted patents.
